# Credits

## Art

[Isometric Fantasy](https://raou.itch.io/free-isometric-tiles-v) by Raou — `isometric_fantasy_tiles.png`.

Free to use in personal and commercial projects.
The author's one restriction is that the art may not be redistributed: no reselling, no re-uploading elsewhere, no minting as NFTs.
It ships here as part of the game rather than as a standalone asset download.

There is no sky or background art in the pack.
Isometric tile packs generally omit it, since a 2:1 projection has no horizon.
